Meet your stylist

Hi, I'm Emma

I've always been someone who loves being creative, and art has been a huge part of my life. Hair became another way for me to express that creativity while also getting to make people feel beautiful and confident. There's just something so special about sitting down with someone, getting to know them, and creating something they love.

One of my favorite parts of doing hair is the connection with my clients. I want my chair to be a place where you feel comfortable being yourself, where you feel heard, and where you know you can trust me — like you're coming to see someone you genuinely enjoy spending time with.

I'm definitely a girly girl at heart — pink is my favorite color, I love all things beauty, and I'm happiest when I'm creating something. I feel incredibly blessed to have the opportunity to pursue something I love, and I can't wait to see where this career takes me.

My biggest goal is simply to make people feel good about themselves and use the gifts God has given me to serve others. Thank you for being here and supporting me while I learn, grow, and build my career behind the chair. I can't wait to meet you.
